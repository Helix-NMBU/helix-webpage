# Sponsor Portal

The Sponsor Portal gives sponsor organizations a managed way to work with Helix and access benefits included in their sponsorship tier.

## Organizations and access

**Sponsor Organization**:
A company or other organization with a sponsorship relationship with Helix.
_Avoid_: Sponsor account, customer

**Sponsor Contact**:
A named person who is authorized to use the Sponsor Portal on behalf of a Sponsor Organization.
_Avoid_: Sponsor user, shared login

**Sponsor Tier**:
The Main, Gold, Silver, Bronze, or Service level assigned to a Sponsor Organization. The tier determines which portal benefits are included and which require payment.
_Avoid_: Account type, user role

**Sponsorship Agreement**:
The time-bounded agreement that assigns a Sponsor Tier and its included benefits to a Sponsor Organization.
_Avoid_: Subscription

**Included Benefit**:
A portal capability or allowance provided through a Sponsorship Agreement without an additional charge.
_Avoid_: Free feature

**Paid Add-on**:
A portal benefit purchased outside the benefits included in a Sponsorship Agreement.
_Avoid_: Upgrade

**Proposal Credit**:
Permission for a Sponsor Organization to publish one approved Thesis Proposal. A credit may be included in a Sponsorship Agreement or granted after a Paid Add-on is invoiced.
_Avoid_: Submission token

**Sponsor Portal**:
The authenticated workspace available to Sponsor Contacts from Main, Gold, Silver, and Bronze Sponsor Organizations. Sponsor Tier controls access to specific benefits, while Service sponsors do not receive portal access.
_Avoid_: CV bank

**Sponsor Portal Administrator**:
An active Helix member who may manage Sponsor Organizations, Sponsor Contacts, Sponsorship Agreements, Sponsor Requests, Thesis Proposals, benefits, and resources.
_Avoid_: Sponsor admin, board member

## Members and talent

**Member Profile**:
A Helix member's self-maintained professional record, including the information and documents they choose to share with sponsors.
_Avoid_: Sponsor profile, CV bank

**Talent Directory**:
The sponsor-facing catalogue of Member Profiles. It is separate from the page where members edit their own profiles.
_Avoid_: Member editor, CV bank

## Sponsor collaboration

**Sponsor Collaboration Item**:
An umbrella term for a Sponsor Request, Member Opportunity, or Helix Engagement tracked between a Sponsor Organization and Helix. The three types have different workflows even when presented together.
_Avoid_: Activity, ticket, request

**Sponsor Request**:
A sponsor-originated inquiry or request that Helix receives, assigns, tracks, and answers.
_Avoid_: Contact-form submission

**Member Opportunity**:
A sponsor-originated Thesis Proposal, Job Posting, or Project Challenge that Helix reviews and may publish to members.
_Avoid_: Job

**Member Response**:
A member's voluntary, non-binding response to a Member Opportunity. The member chooses which profile, CV, and contact information to share with the Sponsor Organization.
_Avoid_: Application, acceptance

**Helix Engagement**:
A defined piece of work that Helix arranges for a Sponsor Organization, such as a Curated Talent Introduction, Recruitment Event, or Technical Workshop.
_Avoid_: Sponsor service

**Project Challenge**:
An invitation for members to discuss a short sponsor-backed project under terms disclosed before publication. A response does not commit Helix or a member to perform the work.
_Avoid_: Consulting project

**Thesis Proposal**:
A proposed master's thesis topic submitted by a Sponsor Organization for Helix to review and distribute to interested members.
_Avoid_: Job posting, sponsor request

**Expression of Interest**:
A Member Response to a published Thesis Proposal.
_Avoid_: Application

**Member Opportunity Board**:
The protected member-facing catalogue of published Member Opportunities.
_Avoid_: Job board, thesis portal
