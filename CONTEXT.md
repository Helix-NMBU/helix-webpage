# Helix member platform

This glossary distinguishes a member's organizational assignments from the permissions they receive in the internal platform.

## Identity and access

**Member**:
A person with an account in the Helix internal platform.

**Helix role title**:
A display title describing a member's responsibility at Helix, such as Department lead. It does not grant platform access.

**Department**:
One of Helix's organizational groups. A member can be assigned to a department through one or more organizational positions.

**Organizational position**:
A job or leadership position at Helix, such as Head of Finance or Project Manager. A member can hold multiple positions, and a position can be attached to a department or span departments.

**Management**:
The group formed by all department heads, the Project Manager, and the Deputy Project Manager. It is derived from members' organizational positions.

**Access role**:
A named set of feature accesses assigned to a member. Access roles control which internal features a member can use and remain separate from a member's organizational positions.

**Feature access**:
Authorization to use one named internal feature. The server checks feature access on requests; hiding a tab does not grant or revoke access by itself.

_Avoid_: using "role" without saying whether it means a Helix role title or an access role.

## Finance

**Purchase request**:
A proposal to spend money, with estimated purchase lines and a recorded approval decision. One approved request can cover several registered purchases.

**Registered purchase**:
A recorded expense supported by receipts or an invoice, with its actual line amounts and budget allocations. Registration does not mean the purchase was approved beforehand or paid.

**Approved commitment**:
Approved spending that has not yet become registered actual cost or been released by closing its request.

**Invoice obligation**:
One invoice amount owed to a supplier, with a shared due date and payment state. Its supporting files are evidence for the same obligation.
