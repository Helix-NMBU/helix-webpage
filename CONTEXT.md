# Helix member platform

This glossary distinguishes a member's organizational assignments from the permissions they receive in the internal platform.

## Identity and access

**Member**:
A person with an account in the Helix internal platform.

**Department**:
One of Helix's organizational groups. A member has a home department.

**Organizational position**:
A job or leadership position at Helix, such as Head of Finance or Deputy Project Manager. A member can hold multiple positions.

**Management**:
The group formed by all department leads, the Project Manager, and the Deputy Project Manager. It is derived from members' organizational positions.

**Platform role**:
One of the controlled access groups assigned to a member: Project Manager, Department Lead, Team Member, or Platform administrator. Members can hold multiple platform roles.

**Feature access**:
Authorization to use one named internal feature. The server checks feature access for requests; hiding a tab does not grant or revoke access by itself.

_Avoid_: using "role" without saying whether it means an organizational position or a platform role.

## Project finances

**Project lead**:
A member assigned to lead a particular project. This assignment is distinct from the platform-wide Project Manager role.

**Project budget**:
A project's planned purchases and their budgeted amounts and priorities. Its project lead submits it for department review; Head of Finance approves its included changes as part of the department budget submission.

**Department budget**:
A department's allocated planned purchases, grouped by project with project subtotals and a combined department total.

**Department budget submission**:
The department budget sent by a department lead for one Head of Finance decision. It identifies the project changes included and the projects that are unfinished.

**Budget revision**:
A proposed change to a budget that has already been approved. The revised contents require department approval and Finance approval again.

**P0 purchase obligation**:
A mandatory budget entry tied to an awarded funding application's purchase conditions or another approved deal obligation. Project leads choose ordinary priorities P1 through P4 rather than assign P0 themselves.

**Purchase completion**:
An explicit confirmation that everything required by a budget entry has been purchased, possibly across several orders. It is separate from invoice payment and the amount spent.
