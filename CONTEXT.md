# Helix member platform

This glossary distinguishes a member's organizational assignments from the permissions they receive in the internal platform.

## Identity and access

**Member**:
A person with an account in the Helix internal platform.

**Department**:
One of Helix's organizational groups. A member has a home department.

**Organizational position**:
A job or leadership position at Helix, such as Head of Finance or Deputy Project Manager. A member can hold multiple positions.

**Management**:
The group formed by all department leads, the Project Manager, and the Deputy Project Manager. It is derived from members' organizational positions.

**Platform role**:
One of the controlled access groups assigned to a member: Project Manager, Department Lead, Team Member, or Platform administrator. Members can hold multiple platform roles.

**Feature access**:
Authorization to use one named internal feature. The server checks feature access for requests; hiding a tab does not grant or revoke access by itself.

_Avoid_: using "role" without saying whether it means an organizational position or a platform role.
